# Product Planning Documentation

Product planning documents frame customer and operator decisions. They do not
authorize source changes, activate features, or replace the design and source
authority for a feature.

## Product media

- [Product Media Architecture Adjudication v0.1](./product-media-architecture-adjudication-v0.1.md)
  — records the human disposition of Fable's clean-sheet product-media
  architecture. It grants no source, migration, media-data,
  customer-publication, checkout-enforcement, or image-similarity authority.
  The frozen [Product Media Contract v0.1](../discovery/product-media-contract-v0.1.md)
  is the design authority, and its next gate is an independent Opus technical
  architecture audit.

## Manual item relations

- [Manual Item Relations Product and Operations Adjudication v0.1](./manual-item-relations-product-ops-adjudication-v0.1.md)
  — adjudicates the post-merge Fable product/operations audit, records the
  source-verified F-10 resolution, and defines the dormant first-activation
  policy and F-09 preactivation dependency.

Product documents must link the applicable design and source authority when
one exists. For manual item relations, the frozen
[design contract](../discovery/manual-item-relations-contract-v0.1.md), the
[source-status/as-built record](../discovery/manual-item-relations-source-status-v0.1.md),
and the [operations runbook](../operations/manual-item-relations-operations-v0.1.md)
remain the authorities for their declared scopes.
