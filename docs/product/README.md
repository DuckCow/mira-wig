# Product Planning Documentation

Product planning documents frame customer and operator decisions. They do not
authorize source changes, activate features, or replace the design and source
authority for a feature.

## Catalog Product Specification and configurable-product foundation

- [Catalog Product Specification & Configurable Product Foundation Contract v0.1](./catalog-product-specification-and-configurable-product-foundation-contract-v0.1.md)
  — establishes the bounded internal product-truth authority, Technical Model and
  immutable Configuration Revision semantics required before C1 source work. It
  grants no source, schema, migration, customer, commerce, manufacturing, sharing
  or deployment authority.
- [External Product Truth Authority Decision v0.1](./external-product-truth-authority-decision-v0.1.md)
  — records that no known external PIM/ERP/supplier/manufacturing master currently
  owns governed product truth and requires this bounded internal authority.

## Product media

- [Product Media Architecture Adjudication v0.1](./product-media-architecture-adjudication-v0.1.md)
  — records the human disposition of Fable's clean-sheet product-media
  architecture. It grants no source, migration, media-data,
  customer-publication, checkout-enforcement, or image-similarity authority.
- [Product Media v0.2 Audit Adjudication v0.1](./product-media-v0.2-audit-adjudication-v0.1.md)
  — records the project owner's acceptance of the complete textual T7
  remediations and authorizes documentation install only after conformance
  review.
Upon reviewed merge, the frozen
[Product Media Contract v0.2](../discovery/product-media-contract-v0.2.md)
becomes the design authority and
[Product Media Contract v0.1](../discovery/product-media-contract-v0.1.md)
becomes historical/superseded. The independent model audit report is review
evidence, not design authority. No source, migration, media-data,
customer-publication, checkout-enforcement, machine-use, or image-similarity
authority is granted.

## Manual item relations

- [Manual Item Relations Product and Operations Adjudication v0.1](./manual-item-relations-product-ops-adjudication-v0.1.md)
  — adjudicates the post-merge Fable product/operations audit, records the
  source-verified F-10 resolution, and defines the dormant first-activation
  policy and F-09 preactivation dependency.

Product documents must link the applicable design and source authority when
one exists. For manual item relations, the frozen
[design contract](../discovery/manual-item-relations-contract-v0.1.md), the
[source-status/as-built record](../discovery/manual-item-relations-source-status-v0.1.md),
and the [operations runbook](../operations/manual-item-relations-operations-v0.1.md)
remain the authorities for their declared scopes.
